# My Favorite Series: Arcane

Arcane is an animated series set in the world of *League of Legends*.  
It features emotional storytelling, stunning animation, and deep character development.

## Main Characters
- **Vi** – Strong-willed and protective.
- **Jinx** – Chaotic, tragic, and unpredictable.
- **Caitlyn** – Smart, calm, and loyal.
- **Mordekaiser** - Mysterious, Best character
  
## Why I Love This Series
1. The animation style is unique and beautiful.
2. The story explores themes of family and identity.
3. The characters feel real and emotionally complex.
4. Because Mordekaiser is there.
